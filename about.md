## Who I am

Security researcher and bug bounty hunter from Argentina. I study Computer Science and work independently on offensive security — finding real vulnerabilities, building tools from scratch, and publishing CVE research.

## What I do

- **Vulnerability research** — I analyze CVEs, reproduce them in isolated labs, and write documented proof-of-concept exploits.
- **Bug bounty** — Active on [Bugcrowd](https://bugcrowd.com/jjjoakkko). Focused on web application security.
- **Offensive tooling** — I build my own tools in Python. Not wrappers — implementations I understand line by line.

## Principles

- If I can't explain it in an interview, it doesn't go here.
- Nothing vibe-coded. If I didn't write and understand every line, it's not public.
- Quality over quantity. 3 excellent repos beat 10 mediocre ones.
- Every real finding is sanitized. Zero data that identifies the target.
